# Mobile & touch

<Lead>Every chart works with a finger as well as a mouse. You turn nothing on: the same edits run, and a few things adapt so a finger can use them. This page lists what changes and what a phone can't do.</Lead>

<Section id="fit" title="Fit the screen">

A phone is narrow. Set `responsive: "scale"` or `"reflow"` so the chart fits the screen instead of running off its edge. See [Responsive sizing](/sizing).

</Section>

<Section id="grab" title="Grabbing small marks">

On a touch screen, each small editable mark gets an invisible target about 44px across: points, handles, thin bars, lines and curves. The mark under your finger wins; otherwise the nearest one within reach does. Large marks and text keep their own size.

A tap may wobble up to 10px and still count as a tap, so `cycle`, `toggle` and click-to-add work with a finger.

In a dense chart, a tap near a mark takes that mark rather than the empty space beside it.

</Section>

<Section id="scroll" title="Scrolling past a chart">

A drag that starts on an editable mark edits it, and the page holds still. So does a drag on the plot of a chart with a plane gesture (`draw`, `sweep`, `nearest`, `probe`). A swipe anywhere else scrolls the page.

A full-screen chart with a plane gesture leaves nowhere to scroll from. Keep it shorter than the screen, or leave a margin beside it.

</Section>

<Section id="hover" title="No hover">

A finger has no hover. Tips and the crosshair show while your finger is on the chart and hide when you lift it, so press a mark to read it. Set `touch: false` on [`guides.tip`](/guides#tips) or [`guides.crosshair`](/guides#crosshair) to show it only for a mouse.

A `probe` previews while you drag and settles when you lift. A tap settles at once.

</Section>

<Section id="mouse-only" title="What a phone can't do">

- An edit gated on a key, such as `when: when.shift`, can't be reached. Don't gate an edit a phone user needs.
- Wheel zoom (`edit.axis.zoom`) needs a wheel. There is no pinch to zoom.
- Arrow-key nudging needs a keyboard.

A double tap counts as a double click, so `dblclick` edits still work.

</Section>
